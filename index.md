<section class="home-section research-overview">
  <div class="wide-container">
    <div class="section-heading">
      <div>
        <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
        <h2>Gas across scales</h2>
      </div>
      <p>My work connects sensitive radio observations with multiwavelength data to understand the baryon cycle: how galaxies acquire, transform, expel, and recycle their gas.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="focus-grid">
      <article class="focus-card">
        <span class="card-index">01</span>
        <h3>Diffuse H&nbsp;I around galaxy groups</h3>
        <p>Ultra-deep FAST mapping reveals low-column-density gas far beyond stellar disks and tests how interactions and the cosmic web redistribute baryons.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Research topics">
          <li>Compact groups</li>
          <li>CGM</li>
          <li>FAST</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <article class="focus-card">
        <span class="card-index">02</span>
        <h3>The Milky Way's gaseous ecosystem</h3>
        <p>I combine H&nbsp;I emission, ultraviolet absorption, and cloud kinematics to study the Magellanic Stream and cool gas entrained in the Galactic nuclear wind.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Research topics">
          <li>HVCs</li>
          <li>Magellanic Stream</li>
          <li>Multiphase gas</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <article class="focus-card">
        <span class="card-index">03</span>
        <h3>Interactions, star formation &amp; AGN</h3>
        <p>H&nbsp;I, CO, and optical spectroscopy show how mergers reshape cold-gas reservoirs, trigger star formation, and feed or obscure active galactic nuclei.</p>
        <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Research topics">
          <li>Galaxy mergers</li>
          <li>AGN fueling</li>
          <li>H&nbsp;I absorption</li>
        </ul>
      </article>
